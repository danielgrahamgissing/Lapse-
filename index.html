About Sleepy Lull

A calmer way to fall asleep

Sleepy Lull blends real environmental sounds — rain, ocean, fireplace, forest and more — with original ambient music to build a soundscape personal to you. Describe how you want to feel, and an on-device assistant mixes the right layers together for you.

Made for your whole night

Adaptive Sleep gently softens your soundscape in stages as the night goes on, then fades to silence — or, if you choose Wake Gently, eases into a soft morning soundscape instead. Set a sleep timer, choose a fade-out length, and let the app do the rest.

Make it yours

Mix your own soundscapes from over 90 sounds and ambient tracks, save your favourites, and build a library of your own atmospheres — for sleep, focus, or simply winding down.

Your data stays with you

Everything you create is stored on your own device. Sleepy Lull makes no medical claims — it's a relaxation tool, not a treatment for sleep disorders.
